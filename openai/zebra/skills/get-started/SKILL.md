---
name: get-started
description: Sets up Zebra for the person. Use right after Zebra is connected, or when a Zebra tool says setup is needed.
---

1. Call the Zebra tool profile. If missing is empty, skip to step 4.
2. Ask the person, in one message, only for what missing lists: the names they appear as in meeting transcripts and chats, their time zone, their country and city, what their work must move now, and which tool they use for tasks (and the list or project where they plan their work), meetings, chat and documents.
3. Look up the national and local holidays for the next 12 months where they live, show the dates for them to confirm, then call setup with the names, time zone, holidays, focus and tools.
4. Offer to prepare their day now with the morning skill.
